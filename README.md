# Global Leadership & Strategy

## Overview

This repository presents applied work in global strategy, international market entry and managerial decision making.

The featured project is an International Business Simulation focused on developing and executing a global expansion strategy for a restaurant business. The simulation required decisions involving country selection, market entry mode, expansion, operating strategy, marketing investment, risk and financial performance.

## International Business Simulation: Market Entry

### Business Challenge

The simulation required managing the international expansion of Desi Cuisine International from its home market in Germany.

The objective was to develop a profitable international expansion strategy while making decisions about where to enter, how to enter and how to manage growth across multiple foreign markets.

### Markets Entered

The international expansion strategy included:

• Canada  
• France  
• United Kingdom  

### Market Entry Strategy

A Wholly Owned Subsidiary strategy was used across the selected international markets.

The simulation required balancing the benefits of greater ownership and control against the financial commitments and risks associated with international expansion.

## Financial Performance

The strategy exceeded the simulation's cumulative profit objective.

### Final Results

**Year 6 Revenue:** $68,645,566

**Year 6 Operating Profit:** $35,077,279

**Cumulative Operating Profit:** $121,534,504

**Simulation Profit Target:** $100,000,000

**Result:** Target exceeded

## Key Managerial Insights

### Market Selection Matters

International expansion requires evaluating markets based on their strategic attractiveness rather than pursuing geographic growth alone.

### Entry Mode Shapes Risk and Control

The choice of a wholly owned subsidiary provided greater operational control while requiring greater commitment of resources.

### Growth Requires Continuous Adaptation

International strategy is dynamic. Decisions must respond to changes in market conditions, operating performance and the external business environment.

### Strategy Must Translate Into Financial Performance

Successful international expansion ultimately requires converting strategic decisions into sustainable revenue and operating profit.

The simulation demonstrated the connection between market entry decisions, operating choices, investment and cumulative financial performance.
